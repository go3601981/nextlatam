02 / Logo system

One mark. Used with restraint.

The Next wordmark is the entire identity. Set in a custom-cut sans with the arrow piercing the x — a visual idea about forward motion. Treat it as type, not as a logo: align it, size it, give it air.

Surfaces.

Primary · on white
Inverse · on ink-1000
Tint · on orange-50

Clear space.

Reserve a clear zone equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on every side. Nothing else lives in that zone — no copy, no other marks, no edges of artwork.

03 / Color

White first. Orange used like a highlighter.

The brand canvas is white. Orange is reserved for accents — a single highlighted word, a CTA, a 6 px bullet. Used everywhere, it stops being an accent. Used sparingly, it carries the entire system.

Italic emphasis.

Single italic word used as visual emphasis in a headline. Never bold-italic. Never italics in body copy except for proper nouns and product names ("Manchester United VR"). One italic per headline; the second italic erases the first.

05 / Voice & tone

Confident. Specific. Never breathless.

We are a consultancy talking to a CEO or CMO. No hype, no metaverse buzzwords, no "revolutionizing." Lead with outcomes. Back them with specifics — named clients, project counts, dates.

Voice axis
Direct over decorative.
"We shipped 100 immersive products" beats "We craft transformative digital experiences."
Person
"We" and "you."
Never "I." Never third-person ("Next helps companies…") in body copy.
Casing
Sentence case.
For headlines, buttons, nav. Title Case only for proper nouns and product names.
Numbers
Use them.
"100+ projects," "10 years," "24-hour response." Specific is more credible than adjectival.

Do good

"End-to-end AR/VR for enterprises that need to move the metrics that matter."
"Twelve-week build. Two 3D artists, one Unity engineer, daily Looms."
"We wire immersive products into your CRM and analytics — never as a silo."

Don't avoid

"Step into the future of customer engagement…"
"Our cutting-edge, world-class team unlocks transformative experiences."
"Game-changing immersive solutions in the metaverse."

Bilingual policy.

Spanish and English are equal — never machine-translated, never with the same line breaks. Spanish copy tends to run ~20% longer; layouts must absorb that without re-breaking. Quote marks: «curly» in Spanish, "straight" in English. Numbers: 1.000 in Spanish copy, 1,000 in English copy.
